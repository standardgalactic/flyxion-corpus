**File Summary: ledger.rs**

**Purpose:**  
This file defines a `Ledger` struct used to track and record the admissibility decisions of training steps in a research software repository. It logs whether each step is *Admitted*, *Rejected* (with a specific reason), or *Repaired* (restored from a previous admitted checkpoint). The ledger helps monitor gradient direction consistency and finiteness, ensuring that only valid updates are retained.

**Principal Ideas/Behavior:**  
- Implements an enum `RejectReason` to categorize rejection causes:  
  - `DivergentLoss`: Indicates non-finite loss or gradient values.  
  - `NoisyGradient`: Occurs when the cosine similarity between full-batch and coarse-grained gradients falls below a threshold, signaling direction inconsistency due to noise.  
- Uses an enum `StepDecision` to represent each step's outcome:  
  - `Admitted`: Step is accepted with its index, loss value, and gradient similarity.  
  - `Rejected`: Provides the step index and rejection reason (either divergence or noisy gradient).  
  - `Repaired`: Indicates a step restored from a previous admitted checkpoint.  
- Contains methods to initialize (`new`), record decisions (`record`), generate a summary of decision statistics (`summary`), and print detailed exceptions (`print_exceptions`).  

**Important Dependencies/Outputs:**  
- Relies on standard Rust features: `Vec`, `Clone`, `Debug`, and basic arithmetic for handling gradients.  
- Outputs are textual summaries of step decision statistics and detailed logs of rejected or repaired steps, facilitating debugging and analysis of training dynamics.
