**Components of the Equation:**

3. **Coherence Liability (\(\mu\)):** Measures the internal psychological toll from resolving conflicts or contradictions, scaled by a different weight \(\mu\).

**Application to Human Psychology:**  
The Master Functional intuitively mirrors human decision-making in complex situations. For example, an employee at an unethical company faces:
- **Base Cost (A):** Immediate loss of salary and effort in updating resumes.
- **Repair Cost (\(\lambda\)):** Potential financial hardship from quitting, threatening survival.
- **Coherence Liability (\(\mu\)):** Psychological tension between believing oneself as a good person while working for an unethical entity.

This model illustrates how individuals continuously adjust their internal weights to minimize total energy expenditure, balancing external pressures with personal values and beliefs.
