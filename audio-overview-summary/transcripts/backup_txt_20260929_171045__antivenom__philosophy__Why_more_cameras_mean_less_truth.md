4. **Reference Class Problem**: The choice of reference class—groups to which individuals are compared for risk assessments—influences outcomes significantly. This could help mitigate privacy violations and prevent permanent entrapment in past actions that no longer reflect current realities.

This approach helps maintain freedom by ensuring that decisions are not hastily made without considering excluded possibilities or institutional biases.
