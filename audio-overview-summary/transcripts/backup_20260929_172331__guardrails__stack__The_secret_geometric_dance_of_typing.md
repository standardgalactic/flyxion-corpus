2. **Tools vs. Agents**: High-end tools are designed so that human gestures directly influence the system’s trajectory with minimal lag (delta‑T near zero), making them feel like an extension of the user's body. In contrast, low-intervention agents—like large language models—operate with massive delta‑T, meaning users input a command and must wait for autonomous processing before seeing any outcome.

Because these gestures preserve unique topological features of individual users (biometric signatures), they offer a higher level of entropy than traditional timing-based keystroke biometrics, making them far harder to spoof or hack.

This shift opens possibilities for new input languages that are not bound by traditional alphabets, allowing expression through entirely novel motor control boundaries.
