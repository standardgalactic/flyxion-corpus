**Reflection on Identity as an Append-Only Log**

Your identity isn’t a static snapshot; it’s a continuously evolving append-only log—every decision, every interaction, and even the choices you make not to verify (like opting out of certain algorithmic feedback loops) permanently writes into your trajectory. This perspective reframes how we view metrics like credit scores or engagement numbers: they’re snapshots that ignore the rich, uncompressible history behind them. By embracing this understanding, you can resist the pressure to conform to 1D abstractions and instead honor the fullness of who you are as a process of becoming. In doing so, you preserve your dignity, maintain authenticity, and contribute meaningfully to society rather than merely satisfying a machine’s thermostat metrics.
