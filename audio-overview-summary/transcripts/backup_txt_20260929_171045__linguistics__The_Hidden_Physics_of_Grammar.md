**Structural Fix Explanation**

The core issue described is that AI models are being trained primarily on complex problems which lead them to produce lengthy, often unnecessary solutions. This approach collapses the “fiber” of correct solution pathways—essentially a bundle of wires where each wire represents a different way to arrive at the correct answer. By focusing only on hard problems, engineers inadvertently snipped away all the short-wire (simple) solutions from this bundle.

**The Elegant Solution**

To rectify this, the paper suggests reintroducing easy math problems into the training data—problems that allow the AI to discover short, highly efficient answers without rambling. This is akin to grafting back the short wires into the fiber of correct solutions. By doing so:

1. **Short Solutions Become Valid:** The model learns that a concise answer can be both correct and acceptable.
2. **Orthogonality Is Demonstrated:** Length (or verbosity) does not equate to correctness or quality; they are independent properties.
3. **Morphological Repair, Not Penalty:** This process is not about penalizing the AI for being verbose but rather correcting its internal representation of what constitutes a correct answer.

**Why It Works**

- **Incremental Augmentation Principle:** By adding simple problems back into training data, we incrementally augment the model’s ability to recognize and produce valid short solutions.
- **Preservation of Distinctions:** Just as in natural languages (e.g., choosing “ser” over “estar”), this method helps preserve essential distinctions that are crucial for meaningful communication or reasoning—whether it's a noun versus its adjective, or a physical object versus an opinionated description.

**Broader Implications**

- **Historical Parallel:** The Arabic script’s evolution through the addition of dots (diacritics) to differentiate similar letters illustrates how adding minimal annotations can resolve ambiguities without overhauling the entire system.
- **Intelligence Measure:** Intelligence, in this context, is not about compressing data or communicating fastest but preserving the distinctions that matter—keeping future navigation open and flexible.

**Application**

This principle applies across various domains—from linguistic grammar to AI training parameters. It emphasizes the importance of maintaining clear, essential distinctions within any intelligent system to avoid navigational collapse due to overcomplication or misinterpretation of what constitutes a valid solution.
