SUMMARY:

The essay examines "YouTubeianism" as the underlying vibration of lack within capitalism, arguing that this system falsely promises existential purity and fulfillment of desire while concealing its inherent contradiction—namely, the perpetual denial of lack. In psychoanalytic terms, “lack” refers to an intrinsic void or deficiency within the self that can never be fully satisfied. By adopting a logic of opposition, capitalism dichotomizes issues into binaries such as winners versus losers, consumers versus non-consumers, thereby obscuring deeper structural problems. This binary framing mirrors Marx’s observation that capitalism is perpetually threatened by its own internal contradictions, which arise from its failure to fulfill the promises it makes regarding the absolution of lack.
