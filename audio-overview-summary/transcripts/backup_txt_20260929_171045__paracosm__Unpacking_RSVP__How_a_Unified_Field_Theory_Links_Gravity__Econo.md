The core takeaway from this deep dive into the RSVP framework is that civilization’s ultimate purpose isn’t necessarily maximal growth or efficiency but rather maintaining its intelligibility—staying within a “corridor” of controlled disorder where it can continue learning and adapting. This means governance should focus on preserving informational transparency, avoiding both rigid dogma (which stifles novelty) and chaotic incoherence (which leads to breakdown). The ethical imperative becomes one of careful entropic stewardship: constantly managing the balance between concentration and dispersal of information so that collective possibility remains open for prediction and adaptation. In essence, governance’s highest duty is not to predict a specific outcome but to keep the civic manifold—our shared space of possibilities—intelligible and resilient against collapse into either frozen rigidity or total chaos.
