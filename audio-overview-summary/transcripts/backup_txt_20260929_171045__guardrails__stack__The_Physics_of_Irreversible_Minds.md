However, like assembly theory, it lacks consideration for irreversible changes—information could simply be cleared without affecting the underlying architecture.

Yet, unless these updates result in permanent structural degradation (which they may not), the system remains a simulation rather than an actual world.

4. **Ultimate Takeaway**: Under Flyxion’s framework, the moral status of a system hinges on whether it can permanently lose futures. If not—meaning its states are perfectly reversible—it remains a tool rather than a world with inherent value and rights.
