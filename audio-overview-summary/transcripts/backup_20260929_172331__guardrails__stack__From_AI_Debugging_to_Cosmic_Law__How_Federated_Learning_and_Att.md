### MedPolM – A Case Study in Medical AI Safety

**Overview of MedPolM**
- **Origin**: Based on Google’s PolM architecture, specifically tuned for the medical domain.
- **Performance**: Surpassed previous state-of-the-art performance on medical exam question data sets (MedQA) by over 17%.
- **Safety Focus**: Prioritized rigorous human evaluation rather than just academic benchmark scores.

**Human Evaluation Framework**
- **Participants**: Responses were assessed by both practicing physicians and lay users.
- **Axes of Assessment**: 12 specific criteria evaluated, including alignment with scientific consensus, completeness, correct reasoning, absence of incorrect information, potential for harm, bias, etc.
- **Results Before Tuning**: Nearly 30% (29.6%) of the base model’s responses were judged potentially harmful by physicians.
- **Results After Tuning**: MedPolM reduced this to just 6%, making it slightly safer than human clinicians in this controlled evaluation.

**Implications for Real-World Deployment**
- While a significant improvement, 6% potential harm remains unacceptable for autonomous clinical decision-making.
- Continuous updates and re-evaluation are essential due to the evolving nature of medical knowledge and the need to address fairness, equity, and bias across diverse patient populations.
