The approach also extends to technology and morality, distinguishing between **imperative artifacts** (efficient, opaque systems) and **complementary artifacts** (transparent, interpretable technologies). It suggests that powerful institutions use institutional gravity to smooth disruptive critiques, preserving coherence while gradually absorbing dissent.

Ultimately, RSVP frames governance as a geometric management of informational flows—maintaining intelligibility by aligning societal structures with their reality tensors. The essay concludes that civilization’s survival depends on sustaining this thermodynamic balance, viewing it as the custodian of intelligibility and resilience against tipping points.
