     2. **Vector Commitment Flow** – directional expenditure of optionality, representing structured action and commitment to a realized future direction.
     3. **Entropy Field** – accumulates irreversible constraints or “scars” on the landscape from past commitments.

5. **Societal Implications & Responsibility**

   - This redefines intelligence as the capacity to bear and act upon history rather than merely compute indefinitely.

6. **Unified Claim**

7. **Final Takeaway & Provocative Question**
