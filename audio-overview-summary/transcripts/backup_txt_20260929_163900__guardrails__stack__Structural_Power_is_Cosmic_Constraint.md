Space‑time expansion is thus a bookkeeping device rather than a fundamental entity.

4. **Ontological Implications:** Traditional ontologies fail because they assume monotonicity (addition of knowledge) while structural logic requires handling *irreversible exclusion*—the idea that certain futures are permanently closed off by irreversible events.

5. **Practical Takeaway:** True accountability and lasting change require commitments that genuinely prune the tree of future possibilities, not merely symbolic or ideological shifts.
