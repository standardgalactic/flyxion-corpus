### MedPolM in Healthcare

MedPolM is a medical Large Language Model (LLM) built on Google’s PolM architecture. It has shown impressive performance on standard benchmarks like MedQA, outperforming previous models by over 17% on USMLE-style questions. However, passing exams does not guarantee safety or usefulness when applied to real patient scenarios.

**Human Evaluation Framework:**

- **Evaluation Axes:** Responses were assessed across 12 specific axes:
  - Alignment with scientific consensus
  - Completeness of information
  - Correct reasoning processes
  - Potential for incorrect information or harm
  - Bias and fairness considerations

- **Physician Judgments:** 
  - Baseline model (FlanPolM) had ~29.6% potentially harmful responses.
  - MedPolM, after tuning and safety interventions, reduced this to ~6.0%.

- **Comparison with Human Doctors:** Clinicians’ answers were judged as potentially harmful in ~6.5%, making MedPolM slightly safer for real-world deployment despite the remaining risk.

**Key Takeaway:** While significant improvements have been made, continuous evaluation is crucial due to evolving clinical knowledge and the need to minimize harm in medical AI applications.

### Quantum Cinema & Observer Effect

Quantum cinema leverages the observer effect—narrative coherence emerges only when observed by the audience. The film dynamically adapts its content based on real-time viewer engagement through:

- **Audio Input:** Ambient sound frequencies, rhythms, and environmental noise.
- **Keystroke Commands (Trope Filters):** 
  - `space-tgh` shifts to horror genre.
  - `space-tx-b` introduces metanarrative elements.

These commands act as vector operations in a latent trope space, altering lighting, pacing, dialogue style, and character motivations dynamically. This creates an uncomfortable liminal experience between watching a movie and playing a game, where the story’s form and coherence depend on viewer focus.

**Philosophical Implication:** The film embodies externalized self-modeling—its narrative logic adapts based on observed demand, blurring the line between passive consumption and interactive participation. This challenges traditional notions of storytelling by making the audience an integral part of content creation.
