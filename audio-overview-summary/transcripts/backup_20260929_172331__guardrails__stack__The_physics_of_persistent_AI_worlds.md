### Core Concepts

1. **Energetic Validity**: The system demands that every action (e.g., lifting an apple) consumes the available energy budget for that specific object. 2. **Two Temporal Speeds**: To maintain stability across a global architecture without recalculating everything 60 times per second (as humans perceive), the system splits processing into fast and slow update cadences:
   - **Fast Cadence**: Handles rendering, lighting shifts, water ripples, etc., which are superficial appearances.
   - **Slow Cadence**: Manages structural topology updates (e.g., load-bearing walls) that require deep constraint checks to prevent catastrophic topological drift.

3. **Four-Phase Training Curriculum**:
   - **Phase One – Renderer**: Focuses on teaching the AI how to convert latent field data into convincing images, learning basic material appearances and lighting.
   - **Phase Two – Neural Proposal Network**: Introduces vocabulary for physical actions (e.g., moving, fracturing), allowing the AI to propose changes based on these verbs.
   - **Phase Three – Physics Integration**: Implements soft constraint projections that penalize any proposals violating physical laws (energy budget, mass conservation). This trains the AI to balance creativity with physics constraints.
   - **Phase Four – End-to-End Training**: Runs recursive long-time horizon simulations where the entire engine operates. If hallucinations occur, severe penalties are applied, reinforcing adherence to physical reality.
