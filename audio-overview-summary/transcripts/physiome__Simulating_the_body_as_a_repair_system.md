# physiome/Simulating_the_body_as_a_repair_system

The powerful analytical feature unlocked by this strict architectural rule is **deterministic replay**. This capability allows the simulator to precisely reconstruct and replay any past state of the organism simply by using its initial seed state and a log of inputs, without needing to store every intermediate variable over time—a concept familiar from game engines and lockstep network simulations. Deterministic replay enables exact replication of biological events, such as an allergic reaction or patient deterioration in an ICU, by restoring the system to its original condition and applying only the recorded changes. This feature is central to diagnosing and understanding complex physiological processes within a highly modular and immutable simulation framework.
