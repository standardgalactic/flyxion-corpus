- **Flow** (vector flow or exchange dynamics) describes movement between capacity hubs—measured through trade data, internet traffic, migration patterns, etc.
- **Entropy** quantifies disorder and volatility; societal turbulence is linked to diversity and sectoral variety via Shannon entropy.

Institutional gravity acts as an entropy reservoir, channeling disruptive ideas into manageable complexity rather than outright suppression.

Ultimately, RSVP views governance not merely as prediction but as a process of preserving conditions where intelligibility and predictability remain viable—civilization’s role is to sustain the “symmetry difference” between intended outcomes and reality.
