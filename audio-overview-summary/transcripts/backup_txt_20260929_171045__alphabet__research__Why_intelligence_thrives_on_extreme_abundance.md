The term being referenced here is "grokking." It originates from Robert A. Heinlein's 1961 science fiction novel *Stranger in a Strange Land*, where the word was coined by the character Valentine Michael Smith (Val), an extraterrestrial raised on another planet, to describe a deep understanding or intuitive grasp of something after initially memorizing details superficially. In the context you provided, grokking refers to the phenomenon observed in AI training experiments where, after initially achieving perfect memorization of training data (with zero error rate), the model eventually develops a genuine comprehension and generalization ability for the underlying rules governing that data—essentially "grokking" or intuitively understanding what it has learned.
