**Flyxion and the Binding Problem**

The binding problem in neuroscience asks how disparate sensory inputs—like visual, auditory, and tactile data—are integrated into a single conscious experience. In our analogy of catching a baseball:

- **Visual cortex**: processes the sight of a red ball spinning.
- **Auditory cortex**: handles the sound of the ball hitting a glove.
- **Somatosensory cortex**: registers the impact on your hand.

These are localized streams in different brain regions, yet we experience one seamless moment. Infection’s perspective frames this as a "sheaf gluing problem," akin to mathematical topology where separate sections (sensory data) must be stitched together into a globally realizable state—this is constraint closure, mirroring the idea of integrated information theory (IIT).

**Integrated Information Theory (IIT)**

**Generative AI and Moral Duty**
