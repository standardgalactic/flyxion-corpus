- **Hilbert curve** for persisting 3D wave data onto a flat SSD, preserving spatial proximity of memory patterns.

The thesis is that true intelligence requires physical embodiment—experiencing time, needing restorative sleep, and interpreting data through emotional resonance rather than brute computational power alone.
