**Final Thought:**  
If boundaries actively dictate what a system can become through refusal—by permanently deleting certain futures—and if true resilience lies in the ability to restore function without needing an exhaustive architectural explanation, then perhaps the most robust systems of the future will be those deliberately designed to remain unexplainable. This challenges our current obsession with algorithmic transparency and suggests that forcing every AI or societal structure into a fully explainable framework might make them inherently fragile. Embracing complexity—where restoration happens without needing a clean narrative—could be key to building resilient systems in an unpredictable world.
