# alphabet/Psychocinema - Arabic English

Summary:

The essay titled "Psychocinema - Arabic English" explores the concept of "YouTubeianism," framing capitalism as a system that vibrates below the universal truth of lack. It argues that capitalism presents itself as offering existential purity and fulfillment of desire, but this is merely a facade. The essay highlights how capitalism denies its inherent contradiction rooted in the psychoanalytic notion of "lack"—an intrinsic void within the self that can never be fully satisfied or resolved.

The author demonstrates that by adopting a logic of opposition, capitalism frames challenges as binary solutions (e.g., winners versus losers, consumers versus non-consumers), thereby obscuring deeper underlying issues. This binary framing exemplifies Marx's observation that capitalism is perpetually threatened by its own internal contradictions, which stem from its inability to fulfill the promises it makes.

The essay underscores unresolved questions regarding how these denial mechanisms affect individual psychology and broader social structures, emphasizing the limitations of viewing capitalism solely through a YouTubeian lens without acknowledging its inherent contradictions.

KEYWORDS:
YouTubeianism, lack (psychoanalytic), capitalism, internal contradiction, binary opposition, Marxist theory, subjectivity, existential purity, fulfillment of desire.
