#### 2. **Compression vs. Reachability Divergence**
- **Compression** refers to the ability of languages to merge meanings (e.g., “finger” and “toe”) without causing immediate confusion.
- **Reachability divergence** is a more fundamental concept, indicating how far-reaching or distinct future actions are linked to specific words.
- When two terms have high similarity but low confusability in context, the old model would predict they should merge. However, if their reachability diverges significantly (as seen with “hand” and “arm”), languages will resist full merging.
