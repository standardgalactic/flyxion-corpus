The core takeaway from this deep dive into the RSVP framework is that civilization’s ultimate purpose isn’t merely growth or efficiency but preserving its own intelligibility—maintaining a balance between concentrated information (which can drive power and efficiency) and dispersed transparency (which fosters resilience, learning, and adaptability). The ethical imperative shifts from achieving specific outcomes to ensuring that society remains within the “entropy corridor” where collective possibility and predictive capability remain meaningful. In essence, the goal is not a predetermined future but preserving the structural integrity that allows for ongoing learning, adaptation, and self-intelligibility within complex systems.
