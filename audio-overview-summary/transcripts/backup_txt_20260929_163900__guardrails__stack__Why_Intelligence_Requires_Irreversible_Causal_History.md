- **SpherePop’s core claim**: Irreversibility in history is the primary mathematical object of computation. The identity of a process lies in its causal path rather than just input-output relations.

- **Mathematical operators** introduced by SpherePop: POP (irreversible commitment) and BIND (contextual restriction), which together model optionality and binding, respectively.

- **Philosophical implications**: Intelligence is seen as the ability to permanently rule out futures through a permanent record of actions. This contrasts with domains where abstracting away details yields equivalent outcomes (e.g., sorting algorithms).

- **Applications & limitations**: SpherePop provides rigorous formalism for systems where audit trails are crucial, such as legal contracts or medical diagnoses, but acknowledges that many high-level computations benefit from forgetting the path to generalize and scale.
