2. **AI's Role in Automation**: AI can excel at automating instrumental tasks by minimizing friction and optimizing efficiency. This means ensuring there are always manual alternatives available so that if an AI system becomes too dominant in a particular domain (like scheduling or social interactions), we still have the option to revert to more traditional methods without losing access or capability.

5. **Auditability and Transparency**: In situations where reversal of actions by AI is impossible, auditability becomes paramount. It ensures accountability and allows us to verify what has been done, even if we cannot undo it directly. This transparency can help mitigate fears about loss of control or manipulation.

6. **The Philosophical Implications**: The broader question posed at the end—whether achieving ultimate freedom through AI means becoming a passive guest in our own lives—is profound.
