The mathematics of continuation geometry teaches us that what appears to be an invariant block is often merely a boundary waiting for the right conditions to expand. Whether it's learning a new skill, pursuing an unmet career goal, or nurturing relationships that seem distant, every effort counts as terraforming. The universe of possibilities remains vast and open; it’s up to us to invest in expanding our own potential.

So, when you encounter what feels like an insurmountable barrier, remember the power of investment and admissibility.
