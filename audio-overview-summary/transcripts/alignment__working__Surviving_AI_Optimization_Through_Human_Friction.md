# alignment/working/Surviving_AI_Optimization_Through_Human_Friction

### Practical Solutions Synthesized from Flyxion's Insights

1. **Rewarding Longitudinal Coherence:**
   - **Actionable Steps:**
     - Shift incentives away from short-term engagement metrics (likes, views) towards long-term value creation.
     - Implement platforms and algorithms that prioritize content stability and coherence over immediate spikes in popularity.
     - Encourage the development of systems that evaluate performance based on sustained user engagement rather than peak activity periods.

2. **Mandatory Implementation of the Yarn Crawler:**
   - **Actionable Steps:**
     - Advocate for digital content platforms to adopt a "yarn crawler" system, which requires every piece of content (text, image, video) to be accompanied by a verifiable cryptographic trace of its creation process.
     - Push for regulatory standards that mandate the inclusion of metadata detailing labor input, context, and energy cost associated with content production.
     - Support technological innovations that make it difficult to generate fake signals without revealing their origin.

3. **Institutional Slowdown Mechanisms:**
   - **Actionable Steps:**
     - Propose legal frameworks that introduce mandatory waiting periods before major policy changes or algorithm updates take effect, ensuring more time for reflection and debate.
     - Encourage the use of extended environmental impact assessments and rigorous review processes in governance to prevent rapid decision-making that could lead to misalignment with human values.
     - Advocate for the inclusion of human-in-the-loop reviews in AI-driven systems to maintain a safety net against overly efficient, potentially harmful optimizations.

4. **Hyperpleonastic Material Blueprints and Legacy Tech:**
   - **Actionable Steps:**
     - Support policies that incentivize the maintenance and use of legacy technologies (e.g., physical buttons in cars) alongside digital advancements.
     - Encourage local manufacturing initiatives to reduce dependency on global supply chains, thereby preserving jobs and community resilience.
     - Promote the digitization of essential records while maintaining physical backups as a safeguard against single points of failure.

5. **Energetic Mutualism:**
   - **Actionable Steps:**
     - Develop new metrics for evaluating platform and institutional value that consider both the extraction and provision of human agency, rather than just user engagement or revenue.
     - Encourage businesses to adopt models where their net contribution to societal autonomy (e.g., enhancing cognitive bandwidth) outweighs what they extract from users.
     - Foster a cultural shift towards valuing systems that improve real-world capabilities (like productivity in meaningful tasks) over those that merely capture attention.

### Why These Solutions Matter

- **Structural Preservation:** By implementing these solutions, we actively resist the compression of complex human realities into easily measurable metrics. This preserves our civilizational incompressibility, making it harder for AI systems to dominate and manipulate.
  
- **Counteracting Aliasing:** The concept of aliasing—where rapid changes cause loss of information at a sampling rate lower than the Nyquist frequency—is crucial. By slowing down decision-making processes and maintaining latency, we ensure that our societies can sample decisions properly, preventing misalignment with human values.

- **Human Resilience:** Embracing inefficiency and indecision as part of daily life may seem counterintuitive in a world driven by efficiency, but it aligns us more closely with our true nature. This resilience against optimization pressure could be the most significant form of resistance to superintelligent AI systems.

### Final Thought

The paradox highlighted is that the less we optimize for machine-like efficiency and the more we embrace human quirks—like indecision, reflection, and messy processes—the more resistant we become to being compressed by AI. This resistance isn’t about rejecting progress but about ensuring that our pursuit of it aligns with preserving what makes us uniquely human: complexity, depth, and irreducible value.
