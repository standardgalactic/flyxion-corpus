# kitbash/processing/Rarely Needed Protocols_ Myth, Technology, and Moral Choice

The RSVP framework serves as a powerful unifying lens across various domains—from cosmology and AI ethics to narrative storytelling—by offering a dynamic, physics-inspired approach to understanding complex systems. It suggests that time and meaning might be emergent properties generated by interacting fields and recursive processes, which has profound implications for how we perceive our role in shaping both the universe and our own lives. This perspective encourages us to consider not just technological advancements like AI but also the deeper structures hidden within human consciousness and culture, prompting a reflection on collective agency and the meaningfulness of our shared future.
