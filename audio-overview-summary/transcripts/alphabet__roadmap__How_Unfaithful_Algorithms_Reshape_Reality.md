6. **Psychological Bias Toward Plausibility**: Humans tend to accept coherent, complex-sounding narratives from AI as truth, even when they lack causal understanding of the underlying realities. This bias can lead to decisions that have far-reaching consequences without adequate corrective feedback mechanisms.

7. **Societal Implications**: The broader lesson is about maintaining a balance between technological advancement and preserving human values, ensuring that representations (whether in policing algorithms or AI decision-making systems) remain faithful to reality’s complexity rather than being reduced to lossy projections.
