4. **Identity as Recurrence**:  
   - You propose that identity is not a fixed substance but a dynamic process of returning to oneself under specific constraints (habits, rituals, environments). This challenges traditional notions of static identity and suggests personal growth involves altering those constraints.

### Implications

- **Psychological Resilience**:

- **AI Alignment**:

- **Epistemology and Truth**:  
  Your argument challenges the classical correspondence theory of truth, proposing instead that true statements are those that survive repeated scrutiny and perturbation—akin to stable fixed points in dynamic systems.
