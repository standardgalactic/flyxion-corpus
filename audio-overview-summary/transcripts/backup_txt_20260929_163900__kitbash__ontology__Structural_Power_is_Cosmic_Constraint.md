**Summary**

The essay presents a radical thesis: power operates not through agency or persuasion but as *structural constraint*—an inherent configuration of the environment that compels compliance. In social contexts, this manifests as the “absence of independent access to means of subsistence,” making survival itself a form of mute compulsion. The authors argue that culture and ideology are secondary adaptations to these material constraints; genuine political change requires crossing a critical threshold (θ) where collective action becomes structurally effective—through counterstructures like strikes or mutual aid.

The framework extends this logic to physics via the RSVP model, which posits that both quantum mechanics and cosmology are *compression artifacts* derived from an underlying irreversible stochastic process called lamfordine flow. This view redefines reality as a single, continuous plenum where objects (stars, cells) are merely dissipative structures channeling universal entropy gradients.

Key concepts include:
- **Structural Power**: The configuration of the environment that enforces compliance without explicit coercion.
- **Mute Compulsion**: Survival mechanisms that compel action through necessity rather than sanction.
- **Lamfordine Flow**: Universal cosmic friction driving irreversible smoothing of entropy gradients.
- **Compression Artifacts**: Mathematical tools (wave functions, expanding space-time) used to approximate the unknowable global history.
- **Irreversibility & Entropy**: Fundamental rules governing both social and physical systems.
