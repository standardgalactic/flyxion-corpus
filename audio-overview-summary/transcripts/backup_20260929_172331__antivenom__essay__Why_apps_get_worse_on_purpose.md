2. **Psychological Lock-In**: Just as the ghosts are reluctant to leave due to internalized grievances and self-conceptions, modern creators often prioritize superficial metrics (like algorithmic views) over genuine connections, illustrating how identity can become fused with external platforms’ metrics.

3. **Contestability vs. Good Platforms**: The central thesis is that the opposite of an in-shittified platform isn’t a benevolent tech billionaire’s platform but rather a contestable one—where structural power allows users to challenge and leave without losing value or connections.

4. **Data Portability vs. Continuation Preservation**:
   - *State preservation* (current data portability) only preserves a snapshot of past interactions, which is practically useless.
   - *Continuation preservation* would allow seamless migration across platforms, preserving relationships and affordances—akin to having the ability to continue using your digital social graph without starting anew.

5. **Hope as Discipline**: Hope isn’t merely optimism but an active discipline that involves creating new possibilities (e.g., advocating for interoperability laws or building open-source protocols) to make alternative futures more reachable.

6. **Physical World Analogies**: The discussion extends into physical infrastructure, questioning how digital rights translate into real-world scenarios like smart home devices and the implications of platform dependencies on everyday life.

7. **Final Thought on Physical Lock-In**: It’s a chilling reminder that as our homes become increasingly connected to digital ecosystems (e.g., smart locks), the concept of “another continuation” must extend beyond screens into physical spaces, raising questions about freedom and control in all aspects of life.

This deep dive underscores how platforms shape not only our online experiences but also our physical environments, emphasizing the importance of structural changes—like interoperability—that can empower users to maintain agency over their digital and physical lives.
