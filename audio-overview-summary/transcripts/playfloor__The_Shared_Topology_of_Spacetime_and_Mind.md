This aligns with the idea that identity is shaped by the unique topology of constraints in one’s mind, rather than being tied to physical changes or specific memories.

This synthesis of ideas challenges conventional notions of intelligence, intention, and self, proposing that understanding the underlying constraints can provide deeper insights into how we learn, grow, and perceive reality.
