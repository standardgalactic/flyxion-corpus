The core idea of Flyxion’s paper is that intelligence—whether biological or artificial—is fundamentally about managing entropy: maintaining a coherent, lower‑entropy “boundary” in an environment dominated by higher entropy. Traditional AI systems have struggled because they either impose too many rigid constraints (leading to brittleness and inability to adapt) or allow too much freedom without proper grounding (resulting in hallucinations and false continuations). The paper argues that true agency requires a balance: we must design systems capable of navigating both stable, constrained regimes (like medical records or nuclear control software) and more fluid, high‑entropy environments (such as social media feeds or rapid prototyping spaces), using appropriate tools for each. Ultimately, existence is an active process—maintaining constraints against the natural tendency toward disorder—and every day we remain “us” is a victory over entropy’s pull toward chaos.
