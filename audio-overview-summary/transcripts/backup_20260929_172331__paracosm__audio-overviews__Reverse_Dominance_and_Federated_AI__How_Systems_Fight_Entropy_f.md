The overarching theme is that cooperation in any system—human or artificial—requires the active suppression of individual selfish signals. Whether through social sanctions among prehistoric tribes or privacy-preserving algorithms in modern AI, maintaining group coherence necessitates mechanisms that counteract entropy and dominance at their core.
