**Title: Curing Trajectory Collapse – A Multi‑Level Strategy Against Map Eating Territory**

---

### 1. Understanding the Core Problem

- **Trajectory Collapse**: When economic utility (income, status, healthcare) is extracted by AI automation, institutions withdraw necessary resources, shrinking accessible futures and life options.
- **Map vs. Territory**: The “map” represents our current narrative about AI development—an arms race—while the “territory” is reality: a complex policy space with many viable options (e.g., treaties, safety protocols). Reducing everything to a single axis (“faster vs. slower”) blinds us to these possibilities.

### 2. Interventions Across Four Scales

#### **A. Phenomenological Level – Individual Minds**
- **Brutal Realism**: Recognize structural field distortions (e.g., automation causing job loss) and take responsibility for systemic economic failures.
- **Reclaiming the Backstage**: Shift focus from external validation to internal growth, allowing space for mistakes and exploration.

#### **B. Economic Level – AI Reform**
- **Purpose‑Driven Deployment**: Use AI to solve real physical bottlenecks (e.g., AlphaFold’s protein folding) rather than as a tool for mass automation that hollows out human bargaining power.
- **Protect Material Futures**: Demand regulatory safeguards ensuring economic utility is not monopolized by AI, preserving the capacity of workers and innovators to thrive.

#### **C. Ideological Level – Democratic Critique**
- **Reject Techno‑Utopianism**: Challenge narratives that posit a single, elite‑imagined future as inevitable.
- **State of Exception**: Refuse to let exceptional crises (e.g., pandemics) justify erasing the rights and futures of present humans in favor of speculative long‑termist outcomes.

#### **D. Legal Level – Adaptive Regulation**
- **Framework by Toxon & Arbel**: Implement *adaptive regulation* using dynamic mechanisms:
  - **Legislative Triggers**: Conditional thresholds for severe safety regulations (e.g., AI crossing a capability to hack critical infrastructure).
  - **Aggressive Disclosure Requirements**: Mandate public reporting on training data, energy usage, and internal safety failures.
  - **Sunset & Sunrise Clauses**: Laws expire after review periods, forcing re‑debate based on new empirical evidence.

### 3. The Final Formula for Civilizational Health

**Civilizational health = Preservation of trajectory diversity**

- A healthy society is not a finely‑tuned machine optimizing projections but an expansive space where diverse futures can flourish.
- Emphasize that the person (human being) is more than their economic output; the future is open, not predetermined.

### 4. Practical Takeaway: The Burnout Challenge

**Question to Ask Yourself**:  
*Am I expanding my actual future possibilities or merely polishing a static map?*

- **If you’re optimizing for efficiency without preserving your generator (capacity to create and adapt), consider scaling back the pressure.*  
- **Ask:** Are you maintaining your capacity to breathe, make mistakes, and explore new paths? Or are you just playing someone else’s performance?

### 5. Closing Thought

By confronting the map‑eating territory at every level—mind, economy, ideology, law—we can collectively resist trajectory collapse and nurture a vibrant, adaptable future for all.

*Take care of your territory; we’ll see you next time.*
