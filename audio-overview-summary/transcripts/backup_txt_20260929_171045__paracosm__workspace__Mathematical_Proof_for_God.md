**de Ballers – A Master Functional Equation Explained**

The concept introduced here is a *master functional* equation designed to quantify the total cost (in dollars) of taking any specific action. This equation balances three distinct variables:

1. **A dollar (Base Energy)**  
   - Represents the ordinary, baseline energy required simply to execute the action.

2. **A (Repair Cost)**  
   - Quantifies the cost incurred when an action lands you outside a permissible set (i.e., breaking rules).  
   - This repair cost is scaled by a sensitivity weight, denoted as λ (lambda).

3. **TA (Coherence Liability)**  
   - Measures the energetic cost of resolving any internal contradictions or “confounded gluts” created along the way.  
   - It is scaled by another weight, μ (mu).

The full equation is expressed as:

\[
D = A + A + \lambda R_A + \mu C_A
\]

where:
- **D** = Total overall cost of taking the action.
- **A** (base energy) = Ordinary base energy required for execution.
- **λR_A** = Repair cost when violating rules, scaled by λ.
- **μC_A** = Coherence liability, scaling internal psychological tolls, scaled by μ.
