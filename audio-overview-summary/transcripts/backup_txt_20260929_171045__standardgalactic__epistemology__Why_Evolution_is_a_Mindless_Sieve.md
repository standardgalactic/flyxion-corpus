The overarching message is that knowledge and perception are inherently limited by individual and systemic constraints, reflecting both the physical universe's structure (like dark matter) and our own epistemic limitations. It invites ongoing questioning of one’s assumptions and openness to the unseen influences shaping others' experiences.
