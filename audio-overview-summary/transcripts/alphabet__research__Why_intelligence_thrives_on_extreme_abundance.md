# alphabet/research/Why_intelligence_thrives_on_extreme_abundance

The term you’re referring to is **“grokking.”** In this context, grokking describes a phenomenon where an AI model initially memorizes training data perfectly (achieving zero error) but later improves its generalization by abstracting and understanding the underlying rule or structure of that data. This process illustrates how learning can occur in two phases: first building detailed “spikes” for specific instances, followed by abstraction to form a smooth, global continuity.
