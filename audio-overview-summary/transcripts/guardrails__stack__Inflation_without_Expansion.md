# guardrails/stack/Inflation without Expansion

Summary:

The audio overview critiques conventional cosmological models—particularly the Cold Dark Matter (CDM) framework—that assume space is expanding due to dark energy. The essay argues that inflation can occur without actual cosmic expansion, suggesting instead that metric stretching explains observed phenomena like redshift and apparent singularities. It emphasizes avoiding unphysical solutions such as infinite density by treating gravity not as a force but as descent within constraints. Key terminology includes “antarpic” (a logically flawed concept), “entropy’s enough,” and the repeated refrain of “no expansion clause.” The essay warns against introducing unseen entities to preserve models, advocating instead for rigorous testing through simulations rather than reliance on prestige or traditional peer review processes.

KEYWORDS: inflation without expansion, metric stretching, dark energy alternative, singularities resolution, gravity as descent, Cold Dark Matter critique, entropy sufficiency, antarpic concept, model integrity, peer review skepticism.
