# alphabet/framework/Reality-is-what-survives-elimination

**Theorem 10.1 – Constraint Closure Selection**

Reality is defined as a *constraint closure*: a configuration that satisfies all local rules **and** remains consistent under any admissible extension across all scales while minimizing residual tension (future work). In other words, reality is the fixed point of the realization operator—what survives decimation.

**Philosophical Takeaway – Construction vs. Elimination**

Traditionally we view reality as being built piece by piece, adding truth incrementally. However, Flyxion’s framework flips this: reality emerges from eliminating every state that creates global friction or high residual tension. It is the residue left after all conflicting possibilities are pruned away.

**Applying This to Everyday Life**

Persistent oscillating uncertainty in your life signals you’re operating under a configuration with unresolved future work—high residual tension. Recognizing and allowing this decimation process can help you move toward stability, rather than merely enduring stress or bad luck as temporary setbacks.

**Key Questions for Personal Reflection**

1. What local optimizations or comfortable habits are actually creating massive global obstructions down the line?
2. Which aspects of your life violate the co‑cycle condition (i.e., cannot be sustained without escalating effort)?
3. How can you let the system prune these incoherent modes to reach a stable fixed point?

By confronting and eliminating the high tension that leads to oscillation, you align more closely with the fundamental laws governing reality—turning stress into an opportunity for true stability.
