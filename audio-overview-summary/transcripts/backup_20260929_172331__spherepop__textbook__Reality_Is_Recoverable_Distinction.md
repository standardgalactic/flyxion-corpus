**Flyxion’s Framework – A Summary**

1. **From Information to Truth**
   - We begin by gathering information (data) and organizing it into knowledge through generative rules that allow us to understand concepts.
   - Wisdom governs these rules, ensuring they align with reality over time, leading ultimately to truth.

2. **Understanding Reality**
   - Objects are seen as temporary bundles of surviving distinctions; identity is relative.
   - Reality isn’t a static catalog of “stuff” but the persistent constraint that resists our attempts at reconstruction (the “refusal of reconstruction”).

3. **Trajectory and Existence**
   - An entity exists because it fits within the allowed parameters set by universal constraints, making existence an admissible trajectory through the constraint space.

4. **Time Reimagined**
   - Time is not a dimension or river but the irreversible accumulation of these constraints into a reconstruction history.
   - The past is fixed because its constraints are locked in; the future remains open as it hasn’t yet been incorporated into the structural geometry.
   - The present isn’t a location on a timeline but an operational boundary where admissibility becomes incorporated constraint, giving us the sensation of time passing.

5. **Space and Geometry**
   - Space is not empty vacuum but the organization of distinguishability—distance measures how much transformational work is needed to map one point onto another.
   - The universe’s geometry (space-time) is called the persistence manifold, mapping absolute limits of what can persist.

6. **Causality as Geometric Propagation**
   - Causality isn’t about physical force but the propagation of constraints through admissibility domains.
   - In the baseball example: the ball carries its mass and velocity (constraints), altering the window’s allowed distinctions, leading to shattering because the glass can no longer be an admissible state in that region.

7. **Existence as Persistent Admissibility**
   - Everything exists by persisting within a set of constraints; existence is about what pushes back against reconstruction attempts.
   - This view implies that identity and continuity are emergent properties of these persistent, resistive structures rather than immutable substances.

8. **Human Implications**
   - As technology reshapes the underlying constraints (e.g., AI altering memory or neural interfaces editing brain states), our very structural distinctions may become subject to new admissibility rules.
   - This raises profound questions about identity and continuity: Could future generations of humans be reconstructed into entirely different dimensions of existence?

**Key Takeaway:** Flyxion’s model reframes reality as a dynamic, geometric construct where everything—from the smallest particle to human consciousness—is an active participant in its own reconstruction against entropy. Our understanding of self and world is thus deeply intertwined with how we navigate and influence these persistent constraints.
