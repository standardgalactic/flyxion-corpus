The audio overview discusses how both ancient human societies and modern artificial intelligence systems manage disorder by enforcing mechanisms that suppress individual domination or selfish behavior. Model updates are averaged and then destroyed, ensuring privacy while still enabling collective intelligence improvements. Differential privacy further protects individual data by adding noise to model updates, preventing the central server from reconstructing specific user information.
