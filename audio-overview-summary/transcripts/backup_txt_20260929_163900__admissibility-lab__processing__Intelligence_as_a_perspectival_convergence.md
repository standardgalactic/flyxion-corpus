**Key Takeaway**

When confronted with assertions about human capabilities or societal phenomena, always ask:
- Is the causal link direct?
- Are all relevant factors considered (closure)?
- Does the claim rely on authority alone rather than empirical evidence (source independence)?
- Can independent, unrelated fields corroborate the same conclusion?

If a claim fails these checks, it likely resides in the “soft periphery” of rhetoric and borrowed authority rather than the hard core of reality. This approach helps distinguish genuine discoveries from culturally constructed counterfoils.
