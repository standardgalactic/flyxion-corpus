summary: The audio overview reconstructs a lost satirical play by Giordano Bruno titled “Lost Book,” set aboard the Ark of Noah. It follows two donkeys (Asinus Primus and Asinus Secundify) who labor in the lower deck while other animals enjoy comforts on higher decks, prompting them to demand equality. The narrative critiques religious hierarchy through a series of comedic exchanges—Vulpes the fox defends the status quo, Porkus Maior the pig belches disdainfully at their rebellion, and Noah’s divine voice ultimately crushes any uprising. The play uses dark humor, wordplay, and allegory to satirize social stratification, with Asinus Secundify delivering a final monologue lamenting the perpetuation of inequality.
