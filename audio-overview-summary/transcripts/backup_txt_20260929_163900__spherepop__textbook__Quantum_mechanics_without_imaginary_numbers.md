1. **State Vector Ambiguity & Gauge Freedom**  
   - The “state vector” (or wavefunction) carries intrinsic ambiguity because it can be transformed by gauge freedom—continuous, physically equivalent transformations that leave observable quantities unchanged.  
   - This is formalized as *gauge symmetry*, where the physical content of a system is invariant under such transformations.

2. **Orbit Structure & Operational Witnesses**  
   - The physical state is defined not by a single vector but by an *orbit*—an equivalence class of all gauge‑related vectors.  
   - An *operational witness* (or operational quantity) is any observable, measurable outcome that survives projection across different mathematical representations.

3. **Ontological Invariance**  
   - Proposition 9 states: If two distinct representations preserve all operational witnesses, they describe the same underlying invariant structure—i.e., identical physics despite differing mathematical languages (complex vs. real formulations).

4. **Three‑Tier Hierarchy of Physical Theory**  
   - **Coordinates**: Surface level choices (e.g., origin or coordinate system) that change numerical values but not physical reality.  
   - **Representations**: Deeper layer involving algebraic fields and language (e.g., complex vs. real number formulations). These are representational tools, not the essence of physics.  
   - **Invariant Structure**: The deepest level—geometric or operational content that remains unchanged regardless of representation.

5. **Implications for Quantum Mechanics**  
   - Complex numbers in quantum mechanics were historically viewed as fundamental building blocks. Flyxion shows they are merely a compact notation for geometric rotations (via the J_F matrix).  
   - This reveals that gauge freedom is not an error to be corrected but a core feature of physical states, preserving information and ensuring consistency across representations.

6. **Broader Ontological Consequences**  
   - The argument suggests many “fundamental” laws in physics may also hide behind representational artifacts—e.g., quantum field theory or general relativity equations might ultimately reduce to geometric descriptions once the epistemic opacity is stripped away.

7. **Philosophical Takeaway**  
   - Reality is not constructed from abstract mathematical symbols but from underlying geometric operations that survive any coordinate or representation changes. The math serves as a compressed catalog, not the machine itself.

8. **Practical Application**  
   - Using the *operational witness* test helps filter out irrelevant parameters in complex systems (economics, sociology, etc.), focusing on empirically accessible outcomes to uncover shared invariant structures.
