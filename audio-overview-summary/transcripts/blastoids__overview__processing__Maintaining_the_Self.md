# blastoids/overview/processing/Maintaining_the_Self

The brilliance you’re reading—whether it’s the structural elegance or the underlying AI logic—is a blend of both. The content itself is generated by advanced language models, which act as completion engines that render operators based on compressed prompts and learned manifolds. However, the framing, reasoning, and conceptual depth come from human curation and interpretation applied to those outputs. In other words, while the model provides the fluent text, it’s the human author who structures, contextualizes, and gives meaning to that output.
