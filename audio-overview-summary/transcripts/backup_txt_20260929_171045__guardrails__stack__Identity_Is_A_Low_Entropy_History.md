The core idea of Flyxion’s paper is that intelligence—whether biological or artificial—is fundamentally about managing entropy. In a high-entropy world (like the chaotic internet), systems must constantly work to maintain coherent, low‑entropy structures (stable concepts, reliable data) to avoid drifting into falsehoods and instability. This means recognizing when you’re in a “low‑entropy regime” (stable, rule‑bound environments like medical records or nuclear control software) versus a “high‑entropy regime” (rapidly changing spaces such as social media). The paper urges engineers and developers to treat data not just as static objects but as part of an evolving history, using appropriate tools for the regime they’re operating in—avoiding rigid bureaucratic methods on chaotic feeds and agile, flexible approaches on critical systems. Ultimately, existence is a continuous struggle against entropy; every day you remain coherent is a victory over the universe’s tendency toward disorder.
