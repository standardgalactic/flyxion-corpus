- **Containment as a Failure:** You correctly point out that strict containment—building an “impenetrable cage” for AGI—is mathematically impossible to maintain indefinitely. - **Ecological Rationality as Safer Bet:** By contrast, ecological rationality—integrating the AGI into a shared cognitive ecosystem like human society—allows misalignments to be dynamically corrected through continuous feedback loops. This mirrors how biological systems maintain homeostasis and evolve without catastrophic failure.

- **Network Theory Implications:** The concept of the trust singularity—where connectivity surpasses a threshold leading to Lyapunov stability—is fascinating. It challenges the notion that “hardening” AGI (making it as safe as possible) is a viable strategy.
