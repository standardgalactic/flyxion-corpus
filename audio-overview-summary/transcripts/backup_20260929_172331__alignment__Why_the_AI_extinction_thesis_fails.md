**Final Thought:** The key takeaway is that while it’s natural to be captivated by dramatic narratives—especially those involving high stakes like AI risk—we must remain vigilant and demand rigorous, testable evidence before accepting such claims as truth. Science thrives on the ability to disprove; without a clear path to falsify a theory or model, we risk being swayed by compelling but ultimately unfounded stories. This principle applies not only to artificial intelligence but to any transformative technology or societal shift we encounter in our modern world.
