- **Reconceptualizing Existence:** Traditional physics treats matter as the container for physical laws, while Flyxion flips this: laws are emergent properties of consistent relational networks.
- **Geometry as Residue:** The three‑dimensional space we perceive is not fundamental but a residue left by persistent admissibility relations that survive over cosmic timescales.
