# alphabet/The Great Debate

Summary:

The audio overview dramatizes a heated debate between Emanuel Swedenborg and Richard Price in 1770 London over competing visions of the “second coming.” Swedenborg presents mystical revelations from heaven—visions of angels, archetypes, and a new spiritual dispensation—that he claims validate his doctrines about scripture’s inner meaning. In contrast, Price champions Thomas Bayes’ statistical rule (Bayes’ Rule), arguing it serves as an epistemic salvation by objectively updating beliefs with evidence rather than relying on subjective revelation. The debate centers on the nature of truth: Swedenborg sees divine inspiration and spiritual awakening as essential, while Price views Bayes’ Rule as a rational, secular method for separating plausible hypotheses from falsehoods—effectively a universal gospel applicable to all rational minds and scientific progress. Both acknowledge unresolved questions about the ultimate source of revelation versus empirical evidence, with each side accusing the other’s approach of being dogmatic or mechanistic. The episode ends in mutual acrimony but leaves open the possibility that both perspectives might hold merit.
