**Summary of RSVP Framework**

1. **Great Inversion & Admissibility Geometry**:  
   - Rejects a bottom‑up ontology where matter dictates structure; instead, admissibility geometry (the constrained stage) determines what particles/matter can exist.
   - Matter is viewed as stable “river channels” within this geometric framework.

2. **Magic Ratio – Chi**:  
   - Balances accessibility and dispersion.  
   - Governs whether local reality remains self‑sufficient (Newtonian regime) or requires cosmological connectivity, triggering the admissibility phase transition.

3. **Phase Transition & Dispersion Obstruction**:  
   - When chi reaches a threshold, local rules fail to scale up, leading to dispersion obstruction.  
   - Dark matter is identified as this geometric “obstruction,” not an invisible ghost but a manifestation of unresolved continuity in reality’s reconstruction process.

4. **Entropy Illusion**:  
   - Challenges the notion that information, heat, and gravity are synonymous; they diverge at cosmic edges where geometry frays (e.g., galaxy rotation curves).

5. **Sheaf Theory & Forbidden Continuation Classes**:  
   - Uses sheaf theory to ensure local patches of physics can stably extend globally without contradictions.  
   - Echocomology measures failures in this gluing process, pinpointing holes or irreconcilable differences.

6. **Non‑Local Indexing & Local Dynamics**:  
   - Non‑locality applies to the indexing (assembly) of reality, not to physical movement constrained by the speed of light.  
   - Global coherence conditions dictate local persistence; a puzzle analogy illustrates this: pieces fit locally but must adhere to the global image.

7. **Forbidden Continuation Classes & Error Correction**:  
   - Configurations that cannot persist globally are termed “forbidden continuation classes.”  
   - Such configurations fail to stabilize, dissolving back into dispersed potential of the field—a built‑in geometric error correction mechanism.

8. **Formalization Roadmap with Lean 4 & Agda**:  
   - The paper introduces a formal verification process using interactive theorem provers (Lean 4 and Agda) to ensure every claim is rigorously proven, preventing semantic overextension or hidden category errors.

9. **Cosmological Implications**:  
   - Considering the universe’s expansion, it may eventually hit a universal dispersion obstruction where chi approaches one on cosmic scales, leading to a potential ultimate dissolution of reality as we understand it—a “cosmic forbidden continuation class.”

**Conclusion**: RSVP presents a radical reimagining of physics as an active process governed by geometric constraints and coherence conditions, challenging traditional notions of matter, gravity, and entropy. It envisions the universe not merely as a passive container but as an ongoing dynamic reconstruction, with profound implications for our understanding of cosmic evolution and ultimate fate.
