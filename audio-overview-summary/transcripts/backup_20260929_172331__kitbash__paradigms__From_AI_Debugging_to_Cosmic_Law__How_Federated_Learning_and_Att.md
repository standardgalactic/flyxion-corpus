1. **MedPolM in Healthcare**  
   - MedPolM is an instruction‑prompt‑tuned version of Google’s Polam model adapted for medical use.  
   - It achieved a 17% improvement over previous state‑of‑the‑art performance on USMLE‑style question datasets (MedQA).  
   - However, raw exam success does not guarantee safety or clinical usefulness.  
   - Researchers conducted rigorous human evaluation using both practicing physicians and lay users to assess MedPolM’s responses across 12 specific axes—accuracy, completeness, reasoning correctness, potential harm, bias, etc.  
   - The results showed a dramatic reduction: from ~29.6% potentially harmful answers in the base model to only ~6.0% after tuning and safety filtering.  
   - Even so, 6% is still clinically unacceptable for autonomous deployment; continuous updates and ongoing evaluation remain essential.
