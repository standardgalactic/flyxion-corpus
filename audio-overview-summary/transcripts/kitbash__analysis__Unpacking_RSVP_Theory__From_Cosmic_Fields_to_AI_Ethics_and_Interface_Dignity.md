This isn’t just about convenience but a strategic design for monetization and behavioral guidance.

4. **Design Principles for Interface Dignity**: The paper proposes principles like making emojis optional, supporting local execution of AI models, offering structured output modes, and creating highly configurable interfaces to empower users rather than confine them to shallow interactions.

This breadth underscores the ambition of creating a unified framework that connects disparate fields.

7. **Philosophical Implications**: Recognizing information as an evolving field suggests deeper unity between mathematical structure and reality itself.
