**Persistent Oscillation & Global Obstruction**

The concept of *persistent oscillation*—where a system repeatedly bounces back and forth without settling—is central to understanding unresolved tensions in complex systems like economies or AI-driven platforms. This behavior indicates that the system is carrying high residual tension, meaning there are underlying issues (or “obstructions”) that prevent true stability.

**Economic Application**

In economics, constraints such as budget balance, resource availability, contractual obligations, and local market feasibility act similarly to gravitational forces in physics—limiting how a system can evolve. The *bias functional* or residual tension represents the future work required to maintain a specific structural configuration over time. For example:

- **Attention-Based Advertising Systems**: These platforms appear locally successful (e.g., high engagement metrics) but require continuous escalation of content quality and targeting intensity to counteract user fatigue, leading to unsustainable levels of future work.

**Dynamic Stability vs. Static Optimality**

Traditionally, economic efficiency is measured by static optimality—maximizing profit or output in the short term. However, Flyxion’s framework argues for *dynamic stability*, where true efficiency minimizes residual tension over time. Systems that rely solely on capturing attention without building structural stability are inherently unstable and will eventually collapse under their own accumulated corrective burden.

**Theorem 10.1: Constraint Closure Selection**

This theorem formalizes the idea that reality is defined as a configuration consistent across all admissible extensions, minimizing residual tension. It posits that reality emerges from eliminating configurations with high future work (or “obstructions”), rather than merely constructing them piece by piece.

**Philosophical Implications**

The paper shifts our perspective from additive construction to subtractive elimination:

- **Reality as Residue**: Reality is not built incrementally but revealed through the removal of all states that create global friction. What remains is a residue—stable configurations that survive decimation.
  
- **Personal Application**: Persistent oscillation in personal life (e.g., stress, unfulfilled projects) often signals high residual tension. The key question becomes: *What aspects of my life need to be eliminated for true stability?* By identifying and pruning these obstructions, we move toward our fixed point of reality.

**Conclusion**

Understanding persistent oscillation as a sign of unresolved tension helps us recognize that many “successful” local outcomes may hide unsustainable global burdens. Embracing this framework encourages a focus on eliminating systemic obstructions rather than merely optimizing for short-term success, aligning with the broader principle that reality is defined by its ability to minimize future work over time.
