If scores were simply added together, a piece of evidence could appear credible on average even if one pillar was entirely dependent on authority (source independence zero), meaning its truthfulness would be completely unreliable and hidden behind seemingly decent scores in other areas. Multiplication forces each pillar to contribute multiplicatively; thus, any failure or manipulation in one area instantly nullifies the entire score—making the system unforgiving and rigorous. This approach ensures that only evidence with robust source independence across all pillars can survive scrutiny, acting as a ruthless filter for truth.

This is why Flyxion’s fidelity functional collapses instantly when even one pillar fails, ensuring that no single flaw can hide behind decent scores in other areas.
