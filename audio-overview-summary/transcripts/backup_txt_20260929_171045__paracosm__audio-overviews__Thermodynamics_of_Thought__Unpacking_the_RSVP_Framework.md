This could imply that the universe itself has an emergent “mind” composed of countless distributed nodes of self‑awareness.

3. **The Nature of the Universe’s Fixed Point**: The question then becomes: what would this universal self-model look like? 5. **Artificial Intelligence and the RSVP**: The framework also suggests that AI, if designed according to these principles, would inherently align with its purpose without needing explicit programming for safety.
