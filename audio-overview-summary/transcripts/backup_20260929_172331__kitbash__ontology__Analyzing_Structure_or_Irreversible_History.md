**Summary**

- The essay critiques the notion of political stability through market compulsion, emphasizing that while structures appear stable, they degrade culture; however, it also defends using these interfaces strategically by building counter‑structures (e.g., organized social demands) that temporarily alter constraints.
- It highlights a broader ontological issue: description logic’s inability to capture irreversible exclusion or commitment undermines identity modeling in complex systems where history is dynamic and non‑Markovian.
