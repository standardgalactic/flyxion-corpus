### The Mechanics of Sphere Pop

#### Visualizing Mathematical Operations

1. **Starting Point:** Begin with a large outer bubble representing the entire equation.
2. **Nested Structure:** Inside this outer bubble, place a 1 (the first operand), followed by a plus sign and a medium-sized bubble containing the next part of the operation—here, a 3.
3. **Further Nesting:** Within that medium bubble, include a multiplication sign and a small inner bubble holding the number 2 and an exponent symbol indicating “squared.”
4. **Execution Through Popping:** The process involves sequentially popping the deepest bubbles first:
   - Pop the smallest bubble (2²) to yield 4.
   - Next, pop the medium bubble (3 × 4) to get 12.
   - Finally, pop the outermost bubble (1 + 12) resulting in 13.

This methodical “popping” of bubbles mirrors how our brains naturally prioritize operations based on spatial containment rather than arbitrary rule memorization. It’s a visceral reminder that understanding is not just about following steps but seeing the underlying structure.
