### **1. Introduction**

The core idea is to apply rigorous fidelity checks—calibration, directness, closure, and independence—to discern true constraints from fabricated ones.

### **2. Fidelity Checks Explained**

#### **A. Calibration**
- **Definition:** In a panic or exaggerated claim, the severity of the threat is artificially inflated.
- **Example:** Presenting a statistically rare harm as an imminent danger to your neighborhood.
- **Implication:** Calibration nears zero because reality does not match the portrayed severity.

#### **B. Directness**
- **Definition:** The argument relies on a convoluted chain of what‑ifs linking mundane activities to severe outcomes without clear causality.
- **Example:** Connecting harmless actions (e.g., playing video games) to catastrophic events through speculative reasoning.
- **Implication:** Directness is low, indicating weak logical connections.

#### **C. Closure**
- **Definition:** Ignoring existing safety nets, base rates, or recovery paths that naturally mitigate the argument’s impact.
- **Example:** Failing to acknowledge preventive measures or historical data that contradict the panic narrative.
- **Implication:** Closure is zero, meaning no room for counterarguments or alternative perspectives.

#### **D. Source Independence**
- **Definition:** The entire weight of a moral panic rests on a single authority figure (e.g., media, expert) rather than independent verification.
- **Example:** A celebrity’s opinion being treated as factual without scientific backing.
- **Implication:** Source independence is absolute zero; the argument floats solely on borrowed authority.

### **3. Convergent Corroboration: The Ultimate Positive Criterion**

#### **A. Definition**
Convergent corroboration occurs when independent, blindfolded fields arrive at the same conclusion without prior communication or shared bias.

#### **B. Historical Case Study: Linguists Jacobson & Trebetskoy (1930s) vs. Neuroscientists (2000s)**
- **Jacobson & Trebetskoy:** Proposed that phonemes are composed of binary features like voicing, using linguistic theory alone.
- **Neuroscientists:** Used brain imaging to confirm the same feature structure in auditory cortex activity, independent of linguistics.
- **Implication:** This convergence validates the hypothesis despite differing methodologies and fields.
