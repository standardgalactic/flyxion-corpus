2. **Action Category (Physical Actions & Outputs):**
   - Objects in this category are physical actions or outputs, such as text on a screen or tangible behaviors in the real world.
   - Morphisms here represent transitions between these actions, essentially how one action leads to another based on environmental and contextual inputs.

   - The functor \(G\) serves as the bridge that translates internal representations into external actions.
