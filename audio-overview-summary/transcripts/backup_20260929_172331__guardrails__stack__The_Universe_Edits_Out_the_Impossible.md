### Core Concepts

1. **Entropic Decimation**  
   - The universe, and indeed the human brain, operate by constructing admissible configurations of information and then ruthlessly discarding any interpretation that violates established physical laws.
   - This process is akin to an editor in a manuscript who removes impossible or contradictory elements before they manifest as conscious perception.

2. **Application to Human Cognition**  
   - Flyxion argues against the free energy principle by proposing that cognition involves more than just minimizing surprise; it requires constructing and maintaining consistent mental models.
   - The brain's internal editor applies an "epsilon threshold," discarding any sensory input or hypothesis that does not align with established reality, thereby preventing hallucinations from becoming conscious experiences.

3. **Cosmic Implications**  
   - Gravity is redefined as an emergent phenomenon resulting from thermodynamic principles rather than a fundamental force.
   - Space-time itself emerges as the geometric manifold stabilized after the universe has decimated away chaotic quantum foam through entropic processes.
