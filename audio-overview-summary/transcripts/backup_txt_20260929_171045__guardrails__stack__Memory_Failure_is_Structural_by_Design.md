This prevents defection by making it impossible for any actor to externalize costs without being blocked, thus eliminating chaos that arises from unilateral advantage.

Similarly, Plenum Hub’s requirement for anti-externality budgets ensures that chaotic behavior—where risk is externalized for local gain—is mechanically blocked, making cooperation the dominant strategy.

This approach ensures that meaning—whether personal or collective—is preserved through disciplined commitment rather than contingent moral instruction.
