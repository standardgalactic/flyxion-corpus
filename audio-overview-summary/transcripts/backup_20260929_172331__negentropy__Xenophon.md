SUMMARY:

The transcript discusses Xenophon, a Greek historian, philosopher, soldier, and mercenary who lived from circa 430-354 BCE. He was educated in Athens under the influence of Socrates and served as a soldier during the Peloponnesian War, witnessing significant events like the Battle of Mantinea. After becoming a mercenary, he fought for various armies, notably Cyrus the Younger's ill-fated expedition against Artaxerxes II of Persia, which inspired his famous work "Anabasis." Later, Xenophon settled in Corinth and wrote extensively on history, philosophy, politics, and economics, leaving a lasting legacy in Western thought.
