It highlights how visibility in markets is often bought by speculative actors rather than being based on merit or product quality, leading to an unfair distribution of resources.

2. **Counterstructures**: These are alternative systems designed to temporarily decouple survival from market constraints. Examples include community land trusts, massive strike funds, and mutual aid networks. They aim to create a new form of social organization (called "Survivaled") that reduces the entropic cost of resistance, allowing for more viable forms of collective action.

3. **Professional Managerial Class (PMC) Failure Mode**: The PMC often struggles with facilitating structural change because they tend to prioritize symbolic coherence and cultural legitimacy over building material power. 5. **Quantum Mechanics Reinterpretation**: Entanglement is seen as a symptom of the universe's non-Markovian nature, where shared historical commitments bind particles together rather than implying instantaneous spatial connections. Decoherence is viewed as the dissipation of this Lamferdine memory over time due to interactions with the environment.

6. **Semantic Autocatalysis**: This concept describes how life and cognitive processes stabilize themselves by controlling entropy export, maintaining ordered structures like DNA or coherent personalities through channeling dissipation. It suggests that agency—whether in artificial intelligence or humans—is defined not by maximal optionality but by the boundaries one imposes on oneself.

7. **Worldhood Measurement**: The framework proposes a quantifiable measure of worldhood based on irreversible commitment and exclusion of certain futures, defining reality as what it is not. This shifts diagnostics from factual correctness to understanding misclassifications of symbols.

8. **Core Takeaway**: Agency is fundamentally about constraint rather than description. It's the strength of refusal that defines self, emphasizing that identity and coherence are built through what one will not allow oneself to do or experience.

This encourages introspection on personal history, values, and future aspirations.
