   - Phenomenological reduction seeks to resaturate experience, emphasizing fullness of conscious meaning rather than simplification.

2. **Category Error Risk**  
   - Misinterpreting computational abstraction as phenomenological reduction leads to a fundamental philosophical error because they serve opposite goals—abstraction for efficiency and control versus phenomenology for richness and depth.

1. **Structural Agents**

3. **Invariant Alignment Theorem**

   - This approach shifts focus from behavioral patches to architectural guarantees, ensuring systems operate within a safe geometric constraint.
