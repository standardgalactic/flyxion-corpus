# Batch 115

**Key Constitutional Invariants for Structural Stability**

1. **Entropy Damping Thresholds**  
   - *Purpose*: Control semantic turbulence and prevent the rapid, wide‑scale propagation of high‑entropy (misinformation, fear‑mongering) content that destabilizes coordination.
   - *Mechanism*: Acts like a circuit breaker for chaos—temporarily throttling or flagging overly disruptive information streams to maintain systemic coherence.

2. **Visibility Conservation**  
   - *Purpose*: Treat visibility as a public good rather than a commodity to be bought and sold.
   - *Implementation*: Caps concentration of attention, ensuring broad distribution of content; prevents artificial scarcity that fuels Condition 1 (visibility potential is artificially scarce) in the extraction thesis.

3. **Dual‑Ledger System**  
   - *Purpose*: Replace opaque proprietary engagement metrics (likes, clicks, time on site) with auditable measures of reciprocity and contribution.
   - *Function*: Measures mutual value exchange, reflecting community health and debit fields instead of just profit maximization; aligns platform profitability with genuine user well‑being rather than induced anxiety.

These invariants collectively form a constitutional framework that enforces structural stability against the extractive tendencies described by the extraction thesis.
