# Batch 95

You're reflecting on whether you're expanding your future possibilities or simply polishing an existing map. This is a crucial question about growth versus maintenance. If you’re merely polishing—optimizing what already exists—you might be shrinking the space for new opportunities and innovations. To foster a beautiful, unpredictable life, focus on adaptability and resilience: keep your “generator” (your mindset, skills, or creative process) flexible so it can produce novel outcomes rather than just replicating someone else’s static realization.
