1. **Heuristic Cues:** Fluency and vividness make memories feel “real.”

3. **RSVP Framework of Autobiographical Memory:** Models reality as a trajectory within a continuous state space (salience, vector, entropy), making inversion and verification impossible for synthetic inputs.

**Implications:**
- Synthetic media exploits two key variables: low entropy (high perceptual density) and near consistency (low obstruction). This creates “self‑reinforcing regimes” or propaganda that deform the constraint surface of reality.

**Defense Mechanism:**  
- Social correction via interaction with people holding different belief systems is essential; otherwise, identical exposure leads to correlated drift and echo chambers.  
- Radical disconnection (temporary removal from synthetic media) may be necessary to restore the brain’s natural relaxation timescale and reality baseline.

**Conclusion:**  
Radical disconnection could serve as a necessary countermeasure in an environment saturated with perfectly coherent synthetic content.
